# Student & Learner User Guide

Welcome to **EnglishBuddy**! This guide helps you navigate your learning journey, practice speaking, ace technical interviews, and earn certificates.

---

## 1. Getting Started & Onboarding

1. **Account Registration**: Sign up at `/auth/register` with your name, email, and password.
2. **Placement Assessment**: Take the initial placement assessment to automatically calibrate your starting CEFR level (A1 to C2).
3. **Daily Goal Setting**: Configure your daily study target (15m, 30m, or 60m) in `Settings > Learning Goals`.

---

## 2. Learning & Practice Modules

- **Learning Pathways (`/dashboard/learning`)**: Complete structured modules on Grammar, Vocabulary, Reading, and Writing.
- **AI Tutors (`/dashboard/ai`)**: Engage in interactive voice and text conversations with AI tutors for real-time feedback on grammar and vocabulary.
- **Placement Hub (`/dashboard/placement`)**: Practice mock HR/technical interviews, build ATS-friendly resumes, and explore group discussion topics.

---

## 3. Certificates & Progress

- Earn XP and coins by completing daily practice sessions and exams.
- View unlocked certificates under `Dashboard > Tests & Certificates`.
- Download official PDF certificates complete with unique IDs and verifiable QR codes.
