# Future Roadmap

This document outlines planned enhancements for post-v1.0 releases of **EnglishBuddy**.

---

## Short-Term Roadmap (Q3 2026)

- **Real-Time Peer Practice Rooms**: WebRTC video/audio peer rooms for live group discussions and mock interviews among learners.
- **Stripe Webhook Integration**: Full production Stripe webhook handler for automated recurring billing events.
- **Mobile PWA Capabilities**: Progressive Web App manifest, offline caching for grammar lessons, and push notifications.

---

## Long-Term Vision (Q4 2026 - Q1 2027)

- **Native Mobile Apps**: React Native iOS & Android companion applications.
- **Live Institutional Cohort Analytics**: Enterprise dashboard for college placement officers to track cohort readiness scores and mock interview completion rates.
- **AI Voice Agent Customization**: Custom voice profiles and accent selection (US, UK, Indian, Australian) for speaking practice.
